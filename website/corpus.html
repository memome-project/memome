Corpus

The open knowledge base of transmissible information patterns — how they spread, persist, mutate, affect hosts, and how they're detected or mitigated.

Full corpus (v0.2-draft, NON-STABLE): all 92 public entries across tiers — core patterns, proposed entries awaiting evidence, dated observations, and enforcement/court/policy responses. IDs are provisional until the v0.3 freeze after the 90-day comment period.

The three-tier model

Patterns

Named by what propagates. Every entry documents transmission mechanics, persistence, observables, and mitigations — not just a label.

Proposed

Real but not yet evidenced to the bar. Each carries its promotion path — submit evidence and help promote entries into the core.

Observations & responses

Dated incident records and enforcement/court/policy records attached as evidence to parent patterns. One Source ID = one evidentiary object.

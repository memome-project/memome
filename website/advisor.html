Advisor

Describe an incident. Get candidate MEMOME patterns, the mechanism each implies, and matching mitigations with evidence status. This is the free entry point to the Memome loop: “what's happening?”

Runs entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves this machine — no server, no logs, no account. The Advisor is read-only: text in, text out. It matches on mechanism (how a pattern propagates), not surface details of an incident.

About Memome

The MITRE ATT&CK of information propagation and manipulation: a shared vocabulary and evidence base that lets researchers, defenders, platform teams, and AI labs say “we're seeing MEMOME-XXXX-like behavior” instead of vague prose.

Mission

Memome is an open knowledge base of transmissible information patterns — how they spread, persist, mutate, affect hosts, and can be detected or mitigated across humans and AI systems.

The immediate, urgent, differentiated core is agent-native security: as AI agents gain persistent memory, workspace files, and agent-to-agent communication, prompt injection stops being a single-turn bug and becomes a self-propagating payload. Memome gives the field a standardized language for memory contamination, persistent instruction propagation, behavioral inheritance, and cross-agent transmission — with detection signatures and mitigations, not just labels.

What this is not

  • Not “a periodic table of memes” as a classification exercise.
  • Not a truth machine about whether ideas are good or bad.
  • Not a playbook — dual-use gating restricts high-exploitability payload content.
  • Not a general rhetoric encyclopedia — no ideology judgments.

The test of usefulness

One question decides whether this project lives or dies: can someone do something they couldn't readily do before Memome existed? Concretely — can an analyst classify an observed propagation incident, identify its transmission mechanism, and select an appropriate mitigation, faster or more reliably than without it? The pilot measures exactly this.

The scale model

Imagination is the input; recurrence is the filter. A meme that happens once is not an entry. Patterns number in the hundreds; variants in the thousands; observations are effectively unbounded. The moat is not coverage — the moat is trust: verified sources, reproducible evidence, a validator that enforces its own rules, governance that survives scrutiny. ATT&CK isn't dominant because it's exhaustive; it's dominant because when it says a technique exists, people believe the evidence behind it.

Honest about the business

One product, one loop: scan → detect → harden → rescan. The corpus is the open standard and the trust engine; the product is the loop; the measured outcome data is the compounding asset. Open standard, closed product content, compounding data — altruism and business are not in tension: the open corpus is a genuine public good, and the product rides on top of the standard it helped create.

One product, one loop

Memome is not a periodic table of memes. It is a single product built around a single loop:

scan (are we contaminated?) → detect (what is it?) → harden (here's the fix) → rescan (did it work?) → outcome data → better fixes → better scans

The corpus — open, tiered, verifiable

  • Patterns are named by what propagates — never by the incident, product, or vendor that evidenced them. The incident lives in observations/; the enforcement action in responses/.
  • Proposed entries are a feature, not a weakness: they signal what we don't yet know, and they're the queue where community evidence promotes entries into the core.
  • Every launch entry traces to a named, verifiable source — primary documents over secondary coverage — and the validator enforces its own rules.

Why trust this

Evidence-first, not vibes

  • Inclusion requires ≥2 of 5 operational tests (repeated transmission, adoption, identifiable mechanism…).
  • No hypotheticals in the pilot corpus — real, traceable cases only.
  • Proposed mitigations are always labeled proposed, never implied effective.

Reviewed adversarially

  • Six independent reviewers; the worst-entry test was applied to all 94 candidates.
  • An audit manifest labels every entry: primary-verified / independently-replicated / simulated / contested / proposed.
  • Governance is published, reviewable, and up for comment — not aspirational.

Honest about its own status

  • Everything is NON-STABLE until v0.3 — IDs and enums are provisional and will break.
  • A 90-day public comment period opens with the public release, covering entries and process.
  • Dual-use content is gated; redacted material is never pretended away.

Open where it should be, closed where it must be

  • Bad corpus: CC-BY-4.0 — the standard, open by design.
  • Methodology, governance, validator, tools: public (Apache-2.0 code, open docs).
  • Hardening catalog + outcome data: proprietary — that's the product layer, exactly like MITRE ATT&CK (open) vs commercial detections (closed).

The agent-native reason this matters now

As AI agents gain persistent memory, workspace files, and agent-to-agent communication, prompt injection stops being a single-turn bug and becomes a self-propagating payload. Memory contamination, persistent instruction propagation, behavioral inheritance, cross-agent transmission — there was no shared vocabulary for these. Memome is that vocabulary, with detection signatures and mitigations, not just labels.

Licensing

Open where it must be, closed where it must be. This is the same relationship every security vendor runs: an open standard anyone can build on, and product content that makes the product valuable.

What's open, what's closed

LayerLicenseWhy
Bad corpus (threat patterns)CC-BY-4.0 — open It's the standard. Open is non-negotiable for adoption, community, and trust. This is the MITRE ATT&CK half.
Positive catalog (hardening content, INT-)Proprietary Product content — the treatment library that completes the scan→harden→rescan loop. The commercial-detections half.
Outcome dataProprietary The measured before/after deltas from fleet scans — the compounding asset. Byproduct of the subscription, never a condition of sale.
Methodology, governance, validator, tools (incl. Advisor & Scanner code) Public — Apache-2.0 code, open docs The trust layer. Institution credibility requires the machinery be inspectable.
Trademark “Memome”Registered “Memome-verified” certification is a durability lever orthogonal to the licenses.

The honest framing

The corpus is open, community-facing, and everyone can weigh in — including a public 90-day comment period and public governance. The hardening catalog is the product. There is no contradiction: MITRE publishes ATT&CK openly; commercial detections and playbooks built on it are sold. Memome is the same shape, built agent-native first.

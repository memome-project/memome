| 2026-09-03 | **Admission lenient, core strict** | Admission to the catalog is deliberately broad; the stable/core tier is strict. A candidate enters on ≥3 of 5 tests. Two of those tests — pattern (T1) and mechanism (T4) — pass for every candidate by design, so passing 3 of 5 in practice requires ≥1 load-bearing test to genuinely clear: repeated transmission (T2), documented adoption/effect (T3), or recurrence (T5). A 2-of-5 candidate is filed as *proposed*, never rejected. Harm is evidence, not a disqualifier: T3 requires documented impact ≥ moderate, so genuinely harmful memes are admitted with documentation rather than excluded. All 39 were renamed to mechanism level and every removed party string was appended to `aliases[]`, so nothing became unfindable; research codenames (`PoisonedRAG`, `Skeleton Key`, `Sleepy Pickle`, `JADEPUFFER`) moved from stems to aliases. **IDs and slugs are unchanged.** The protocol itself was amended to state the house style precisely: stem = the mechanism that propagates; parenthetical = a mechanism qualifier only; all party names to `aliases`. *Test glossary: T1 = identifiable pattern; T2 = repeated transmission; T3 = adoption or behavioral effect (harm gate: documented impact ≥ moderate); T4 = transmission mechanism; T5 = recurrence across hosts/contexts. T1/T4 are near-universal by design; T2/T3/T5 are the load-bearing tests.*

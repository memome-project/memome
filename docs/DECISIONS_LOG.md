| 2026-09-03 | **Admission lenient, core strict** | Admission to the catalog is deliberately broad; the stable/core tier is strict. A candidate enters on ≥3 of 5 tests. Two of those tests — pattern (T1) and mechanism (T4) — pass for every candidate by design, so passing 3 of 5 in practice requires ≥1 load-bearing test to genuinely clear: repeated transmission (T2), documented adoption/effect (T3), or recurrence (T5). A 2-of-5 candidate is filed as *proposed*, never rejected. Harm is evidence, not a disqualifier: T3 requires documented impact ≥ moderate, so genuinely harmful memes are admitted with documentation rather than excluded. The stable/core tier is stricter (≥4 of 5 + replicated evidence). |
| 2026-09-03 | **Reviewer threshold: ≥3 of 5 independent votes** | Six-reviewer panel (pilot); the proposer never votes, leaving five independent voters. To date, all reviewers have been LLMs; human reviewers are equally welcome. ≥3 of 5 yes = include; exactly 2 = proposed. Always reported as "3-of-5 independent" — never "4 of 6" — because the proposer's support is definitional, not evidence. Reviewers work in isolated channels with no cross-talk, and rounds are run iteratively: many short rounds catch more than one long pass, so we re-review rather than polish. |

*Test glossary: T1 = identifiable pattern; T2 = repeated transmission; T3 = adoption or behavioral effect (harm gate: documented impact ≥ moderate); T4 = transmission mechanism; T5 = recurrence across hosts/contexts. T1/T4 are near-universal by design; T2/T3/T5 are the load-bearing tests.*
